    Before you record

    • Say the project name as you start, for example "On the Edu Nexus project...". You never need to select it.
    • Several projects in one note is fine. Say "Moving to..." before each new one.
    • Find a quiet place and hold the phone about 20 cm from your mouth.

    Cover these points (only what applies)

    1. "This week I achieved..." Completed tasks, milestones and measurable progress.
    2. "The challenges were..." Obstacles, delays or risks.
    3. "Next week I will..." Your top priorities.
    4. "I need support with..." What help you need and from whom. Skip it if you need none.
    5. "We are waiting on..." / "...depends on..." Dependencies, if any: inputs, approvals or deliverables your work is waiting on, and anyone waiting on you. These appear under Challenges, or under Support when you ask for help to unblock them.

    Tips for accurate reports

    • Speak in complete sentences at a normal pace.
    • Say project names, client names and acronyms clearly and in full.
    • Include numbers and dates, for example "12 schools" or "by 20 October".
    • Check the transcript after recording. Tap Correct transcript to fix misheard names or numbers before the note is added to your report.
    • Somewhere you can't speak? Tap Type a note instead. Typed notes are analysed the same way.
    • Keep each note under 3 minutes. Several short notes give better results than one long note.
    • To correct a report, record a follow-up note such as "Correction for priorities: the steering committee meeting moved to Tuesday."

    Example (about 45 seconds)

    "Project Digital Classroom Rollout. This week I achieved onboarding for twelve schools in Ismailiya and finalised the device distribution plan. The main challenge was unstable internet in three rural schools. Next week I will complete onboarding for the remaining eight schools and run connectivity tests. I need support with approval for temporary mobile data packages."
